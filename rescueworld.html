What is real and what is invented

The earthquake, terrain, mapped landslides, road closures, shelter designations, aftershocks, and public-response milestones come from public records. The shelter list records official designations only; it does not show whether each shelter opened or remained usable. The buildings show pre-earthquake context from Japan's open 3D city model.

The AI rescue proposals were generated later for this exercise and were never carried out. The glowing damage effects are also simulated. This viewer does not model what would have happened after an AI proposal, and it does not claim that any proposal saved people or improved the real response. —
